## Scope and limitations

Math spans use literal dollar, bracket and parenthesis delimiters, common math environments, and ensuremath. Text wrappers are parsed with balanced braces; nested explicit math in those wrappers is scanned separately. Recognized math alphabet arguments and font declarations are excluded from ordinary-capital candidates. Arrow-option capitals, unparsable wrappers and delimiter anomalies are reported separately. Arbitrary macro expansion, conditional TeX execution, verbatim environments and custom syntax are not evaluated; this is a lexical candidate inventory, not a certificate of every rendered glyph. Printed-page numbers are supplied only where source markers encode them; English component locations remain exact source line/column positions.

Compact output retains full exact source text only for capital candidates, parser uncertainties, and math-font commands without detected explicit nested math. Valid nested text-wrapper formulas are represented by counts, not duplicate full-source extracts. Page counts are lexical event counts; English printed pages are blank where they are not encoded. Locator tables retain original data-row IDs, file/layer, exact locators, statement, kind and confidence; old/new/evidence text remains in the authoritative ledger. Generic-locator reasons are lexical signals; only the primary reader can refine them against the print. Verbose duplicate tables can be regenerated outside the deliverable with --verbose.
