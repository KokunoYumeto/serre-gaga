## Coverage and reproducibility

`recurrence/page_pattern_counts.tsv` records counts for every printed page, including zero-count pages. `recurrence/` also contains the compact lexical inventories, source snapshot hashes and terminology/glossary extracts. The scripts document delimiter and macro-expansion limitations. Counts of word matches can overlap; they are not counts of distinct mathematical statements.

The original 2,386 correction records were all reread. Eight further records arise from the three page-boundary repairs (three layers at pp. 8–9, three at pp. 3–4, two at pp. 26–27), making 2,394 data records before build-only changes. All eight have their own second-reading dispositions. The record for the unnecessary addition of “new” in the English p. 19 passage is explicitly withdrawn and has identical old/new text; it is retained to make the decision history visible. Two title-period rows carry medium confidence; the remaining rows carry high confidence, including the explicit withdrawn disposition.

The recurring-error search was completed before the edition build. The subsequent PDF inspection is recorded in `PDF_CHECK.md`; successful compilation was not treated as evidence for an uninspected reading. That inspection produced two further French preamble records, restoring the printed footnote-marker formatting. Both were second-read, bringing the final ledger to 2,396 records. The article-body recurrence conclusions above are unchanged.
